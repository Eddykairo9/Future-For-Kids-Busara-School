# **App Name**: Busara Kids Hub

## Core Features:

- Homepage with NGO Info: Display information about the organization's mission and values, using a clean, impactful layout.
- Programs Showcase: Showcase programs and initiatives, with an emphasis on the future goals for children.
- Impact Gallery: A visually engaging gallery of photos and videos highlighting the organization's work and impact, as presented in the reference materials.
- Contact Form: Simple form for visitors to submit inquiries.
- AI Content Suggestions: AI-powered tool that suggests relevant news articles and research related to education and child development based on keywords, improving website SEO.

## Style Guidelines: 

- Primary color: Use a warm, inviting green (#4CAF50) to represent growth and hope.
- Secondary colors: Use a calming blue (#2196F3) for trust and stability.
- Accent: Yellow (#FFEB3B) to highlight important information and call-to-action buttons.
- Clear, readable typography, sans-serif for body text to ensure readability.
- Simple, modern icons for easy navigation and visual interest.
- Clean, modern, and responsive layout for optimal viewing on all devices, inspired by MIT and Harvard's websites.
